# Employee Data Privacy Policy (POL0010217)

This policy outlines how our organization handles and protects sensitive internal user data.

### 1. Data Classification Levels

| Data Type | Sensitivity | Retention Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Employee Names | Low | 5 Years |
| Financial / Payroll | High | 7 Years |
| Medical Records | Critical | Permanent |
